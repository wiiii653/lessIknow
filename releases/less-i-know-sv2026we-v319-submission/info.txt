THE LESS I KNOW THE BETTER
Silly Venture 2026 WE

AUTHOR
Ognjen

CATEGORY
Atari XL/XE Demo Standard

REQUIRED CONFIGURATION
PAL Atari XL/XE, 1 MB Rambo-compatible expansion (64 x 16 KB banks),
stereo POKEY (2 chips), GTIA. No VBXE or Rapidus required.

DESCRIPTION
A full-length Atari XE audiovisual demo: neon arena scenes, basketball,
ribbon choreography, Trevor's comic punch impacts, banana rotozooms,
psychedelic tunnels, a close-up of the girl's eyes, and a quiet terminal
outro. Native ANTIC/GTIA bitmap scenes, player/missile graphics, bank-switched
art and raster palette effects accompany a live POKEY performance.

PRODUCTION AND AI DISCLOSURE
Two weeks of day-and-night production with intensive collaboration with AI
agents. AI tools assisted with programming and image assets; creative
direction and final decisions by Ognjen.

MUSIC AND RIGHTS
POKEY arrangement by Ognjen, based on "The Less I Know The Better" by
Tame Impala. The author confirms permission to use the MIDI score and
composition. No samples from the commercial recording are used.

PUBLICATION
The author reports that the demo has only been shared privately before this
submission.
