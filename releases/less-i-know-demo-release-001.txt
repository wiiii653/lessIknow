Source: visual-prototype-v254, unchanged.
Target: PAL Atari XE with 1 MB expanded RAM and stereo POKEY.

This is a complete-playthrough build, from loading screen through the final
fade. It includes the eye close-up and credits. The planned Trevor mosaic,
comic love-triangle and giant-Trevor scenes are not implemented; the existing
rotator/tunnel montage fills those intervals. The reported Trevor ball-flight
visual errors remain unresolved. This build has not been checked on real Atari
hardware or visually verified during loading in an emulator.

Validation: v253 passed 196/196 automated tests and full-track stereo POKEY
comparison with v035 in Atari800 PAL/1088XE. V254 changes only 19 bytes of
loading-screen text in the $0600-$0677 segment; its focused loading-screen
test passed. This release XEX is byte-identical to v254.
