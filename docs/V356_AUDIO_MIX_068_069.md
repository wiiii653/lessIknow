# V356 — POKEY mix correction in releases 068 and 069

The starting point is the image and edit of v356. The stereo version 068 tunes
both basses to the left voice in order to remove the detuning introduced in
v035. The stored levels of each chip are limited to a sum of 24 only during loud
overlays, against a maximum of 30 before. This is additional level headroom, not
a measurement of analogue clipping.

Mono 069 keeps the main melody voice throughout its activity and takes the
right voice in the gaps. The percussion keeps the selected side during the
envelope. A new, louder attack from the other side may take over the channel.
Both bass levels combine into one voice with the left tuning. The total volume
on the chip does not exceed 24. The older mono 067 could change sides at the
mere crossing of levels, which caused jumps in pitch or in the kind of noise.

The generator `tools/build_audio_revision.py` reads the frozen v356 prototype.
The packets are encoded in the same format and packed by loader 058. Both
versions keep the player, the number of updates, the 64-bank layout and the
graphics data. The stream occupies 116 582 B (stereo) or 106 785 B (mono) within
the existing limit of 117 520 B. The music data of the first eight banks and the
dictionary `$2E00/$2E80/$2F00` change. In mono, the POKEY writes in the code
`$2000–$24B2` and `$2C00–$2C80` also change. The original levels for the mono
visual indicators remain in the unused registers of the right-voice buffer.
