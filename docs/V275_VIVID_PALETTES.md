# V275 — more vivid palettes and yellow bananas

The bananas in both renderers use the PAL shades `$F8/$FA` and bright `$FE`;
the background colour and tunnel palettes still change at the existing rhythm.
The school scenes, basketball and dance have lighter pink, cyan and skin
light; in the fight Trevor's shades were brightened. The frames and the finale scene do not
change the timing or the bank layout.

`releases/less-i-know-demo-release-013-stereo.xex` uses the compact
loader with late low-memory write. Further builds are to be stereo only,
in line with the user's wish.
