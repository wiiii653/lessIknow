# V280 — ball at the hand and v177 pose proportions

The v177 poses are scaled and positioned with the raster proportions in mind;
the dribbling boy stands on the parquet, and the successive take-off poses rise
gradually. The ball has a new path: on the bounce it follows under the hand and
reaches the parquet, during the dunk the positions rest on the hands of the
successive poses, and after contact with the backboard it moves down. The scene
timing, the backboard stages and the satellites are preserved.

[READ] In the key poses the test measures the distance from the ball centre to
the opaque pixels of the character's right edge. The tests also check the
frames, the PMG position, the scene boundaries, the banks and the music
interrupts. Atari800 PAL/1088XE: 16 311 600 identical stereo POKEY bytes against
v035; the comparison covers the whole track.
[READ] Full test suite: 202/202 in 144.504 s.

Stereo release: `releases/less-i-know-demo-release-017-stereo.xex`.
[LIMITATION] The preview and the CPU tests do not prove actual smoothness or the
artistic reception on physical Atari; this build requires a trial on hardware.
