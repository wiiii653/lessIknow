# V125 — caption colour cycle

[READ] Four letter colour schemes change according to the beats computed from the music counter. The shadow and the plasma colours and phases keep separate values. The colour tables are located in the free ends of four plasma banks; no banks were added. A beat change forces a redraw even in the same plasma phase.

[CONCLUSION] The display happens after the frame composition is completed, so the actual visual delay depends on its execution time. The software preview does not replace listening and observation on the target hardware.
