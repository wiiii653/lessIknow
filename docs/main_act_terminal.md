# Main-act terminal

Source [main_act_terminal.py](../tools/main_act_terminal.py).

The agent types on the bottom row as each action begins. Enter scrolls the
previous lines up and the last four rows carry across cuts. The single
text ink eases into the next scene's color independently of the text.
The credits terminal takes over after the montage.

### 1. Atari Arena arrival

```text
> ATARI ARENA. TRACKING ARRIVAL.
> TRACKING HER IN CLOSE-UP.
> BACK TO THE BOY.
> SHE WALKS PAST.
> THE BOY WHISTLES AS SHE WALKS AWAY.
> AT THE DOORWAY.
```

### 2. Basketball practice

```text
> CLOSE-UP. HE HOLDS THE BALL.
> HE SHOOTS. TRACKING THE BALL.
> STRETCHING AT THE WALL BARS.
```

### 3. Single dancer in profile

```text
> TRACKING A SINGLE DANCER.
> RIBBONS IN PROFILE. COURT SCROLLS.
```

### 4. First tunnels

```text
> SPIRAL TUNNEL.
> ORGANIC TUNNEL.
> SPIRAL REVERSES.
> ORGANIC REVERSES.
> BACK TO SPIRAL.
> BACK TO ORGANIC.
> THE BALL KEEPS MOVING FORWARD.
```

### 5. Ribbon dance

```text
> TWO DANCERS FLANK THE LEAD.
> CUTTING TO THE LEAD DANCER ALONE.
> THE TRIO RETURNS.
> LIFT.
> A KISS.
```

### 6. Tunnel cutaway

```text
> BACK THROUGH THE TUNNEL TO THE COURT.
```

### 7. Arena and dunk

```text
> TRACKING THE DRIBBLE IN THE ARENA.
> FOUR SATELLITES ORBIT THE BALL.
> TRACKING THE DUNK.
> CRACK
```

### 8. Trevor

```text
> TREVOR EMERGES FROM THE DARK.
> CHECKING TREVOR IN THE CLOSER SHOT.
> TRACKING EACH IMPACT AND REBOUND.
> THE PUNCHES SWITCH HANDS.
```

### 9. Post-Trevor montage

```text
> THE BANANAS ROTATE INTO VIEW.
> FOLLOWING THE RETURN TO THE SPIRAL.
> THE TUNNEL SWITCHES TO ORGANIC.
> THE BANANAS RETURN TO THE BEAT.
> SPIRAL AGAIN. BALL STILL MOVING.

```

### 10. Eyes and final passes

```text

> FOLLOWING THE LAST BANANA PASS.
> ONE LAST SPIRAL BEFORE THE CREDITS.
```
