# Main-act terminal

Source [main_act_terminal.py](../tools/main_act_terminal.py).

The agent types on the bottom row as each action begins. Enter scrolls the
previous lines up and the last four rows carry across cuts. The palette eases
into the next scene's colors independently of the text. The credits terminal
takes over after the montage.

### 1. School arrival

```text
> TRACKING THE SCHOOL ARRIVAL.
> CUT TO THE GIRL IN CLOSE-UP.
> BACK OUTSIDE.
> SHE WALKS PAST.
> THE BOY WHISTLES AS SHE WALKS AWAY.
> AT THE DOORWAY.
```

### 2. Basketball practice

```text
> TRACKING THE WIDE PRACTICE SHOT.
> CHECKING THE BALL AGAINST THE HAND.
> STRETCHING AT THE WALL BARS.
```

### 3. Side dancers

```text
> TRACKING A SINGLE DANCER.
> THE GYM SCROLLS BEHIND HER.
> FOLLOWING HER RIBBONS IN PROFILE.
```

### 4. First tunnels

```text
> SPIRAL TUNNEL.
> ORGANIC TUNNEL.
> TUNNEL REVERSES. BALL MOVES ON.
> TRACING THE BALL THROUGH THE CUTS.
```

### 5. Ribbon dance

```text
> TWO DANCERS FLANK THE LEAD.
> THE TRIO MOVES THROUGH THE POSES.
> CUTTING TO THE LEAD DANCER ALONE.
> THE TRIO RETURNS FOR THE LIFT.
> TRACKING THE RIBBON BETWEEN POSES.
> A KISS.
```

### 6. Tunnel cutaway

```text
> BACK THROUGH THE TUNNEL TO THE COURT.
```

### 7. Arena and dunk

```text
> TRACKING THE DRIBBLE IN THE ARENA.
> FOUR SATELLITES ORBIT THE BALL.
> TRACKING THE WIND-UP AND LEAP.
> CRACK.
```

### 8. Trevor

```text
> TREVOR EMERGES IN THE DARK GYM.
> CHECKING TREVOR IN THE CLOSER SHOT.
> TRACKING EACH IMPACT AND REBOUND.
> THE PUNCHES SWITCH HANDS.
```

### 9. Post-Trevor montage

```text
> THE BANANAS ROTATE INTO VIEW.
> COMPARING THE BANANA ROTATIONS.
> FOLLOWING THE RETURN TO THE SPIRAL.
> THE TUNNEL SWITCHES TO ORGANIC.
> THE BANANAS RETURN TO THE BEAT.
> SPIRAL AGAIN. BALL STILL MOVING.
> WATCHING THE ORGANIC TUNNEL TURN.
```

### 10. Eyes and final passes

```text
> THE BANANAS FILL THE FRAME AGAIN.
> HOLDING THE CLOSE-UP ON HER EYES.
> FOLLOWING THE LAST BANANA PASS.
> ONE LAST SPIRAL BEFORE THE CREDITS.
```
