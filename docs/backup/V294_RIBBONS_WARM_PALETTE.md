# V294 — warmer ribbon and ejection scene

The entire ribbon sequence, from the side shot through the group and solo dance
to the ejection, uses the image palette `$38/$68/$2C`: darker reds and violets
plus a warm skin tone. The four ribbon players keep an independent colour cycle
in four raster bands. Brightnesses 10/12 keep stronger colours
against the darker hall. The white and cyan streaks in flight and the heart
colours remain recognisable.

[READ] Stereo release 031 uses the compact AVG loader. The group and ejection
previews were reviewed. Tests of buffers, ribbons, grips, flight and heart:
27/27. Atari800: 15 922 584 identical stereo POKEY bytes relative to v035.

[LIMITATION] The callback preview uses approximate colours. The new build was
not checked on physical Atari; colour reception depends on the PAL path and the screen.
