# V338 — BUM/BAM on the banana rotators

In the post-Trevor director the ball appears only in the tunnels. On the
banana phases, on every beat BUM and BAM alternate, with the same PMG renderer,
letters and satellites as on Trevor's hits. The effect lasts 80 ticks
of the beat, after which the PMG goes dark until the next hit. No new banks and no
timeline change.

Stereo build: `build/visual-prototype-v338.xex`; SHA-256:
`6ba0053d9fbf2ddac4c5837466e02f7da6caadcc9c951ecb1997b6af2aa95e0c`.
The build reports 298 shared rows and 2560 B of code. No tests, emulator or
Atari were run; v330 remains the last release confirmed
on hardware.
